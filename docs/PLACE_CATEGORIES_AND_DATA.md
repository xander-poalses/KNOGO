# Place Categories And Data

Planning reference for expanding KNOGO's place directory. This is not a record of database or app changes.

## Categories

Each place has one category and a type. Features cover overlaps, such as a fuel station with a convenience store. Branches belong to a group where applicable.

| Category | Place types |
| --- | --- |
| Food & Drink | Restaurants, cafes, takeaways, fast-food outlets, bars, pubs |
| Shops | Supermarkets, convenience stores, clothing stores, pharmacies, hardware stores, electronics stores, shopping centres |
| Cars & Garages | Fuel stations, mechanics and repair workshops, tyre and fitment centres, auto electricians, panel beaters, car washes, vehicle testing centres, parts stores |
| Public Services | Home Affairs, licensing centres, municipal offices, post offices, libraries, public service centres |
| Entertainment | Cinemas, theatres, museums, galleries, attractions, gaming venues |
| Sport & Outdoors | Golf courses, driving ranges, MTB trail centres, climbing centres, gyms, sports grounds, swimming pools, parks, nature reserves |

Priority: Shops, Cars & Garages and Public Services, followed by Entertainment and Sport & Outdoors. Shops reflects survey demand; Public Services includes the lecturer's request. Food & Drink extends the directory we have.

## Research

Use the place's website, its operator or another source we can cite. Record gaps instead of guessing.

| Scope | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Every place: identity | Name, category, type, branch, group |
| Every place: location | Address, area, coordinates, entrance where needed |
| Every place: contact | Phone, website, contact and booking links |
| Every place: hours | Hours by day, service hours, published holiday changes |
| Every place: services | Services offered, booking requirements |
| Every place: access | Published parking, wheelchair access, entry rules, fees |
| Every place: provenance | Source link, date checked, information gaps |
| Food & Drink | Cuisine, menu link, kitchen hours, takeaway, delivery, reservations |
| Shops | Retail type, departments, collection options, branch hours |
| Cars & Garages | Fuel brand and grades, workshop services, shop hours, pumps, air, booking options |
| Public Services | Services offered, appointment links, requirements, service hours, queue cut-off rules |
| Entertainment | Programme or ticket link, session times, bookings, age and entry rules |
| Sport & Outdoors | Activities, routes and facilities, published difficulty, equipment hire, bookings, access fees, weather closure rules |

Store hours by service when they differ: a garage forecourt and its shop, or a building and its counters, may not share hours. Prices, schedules and requirements need a source and date; link to the source rather than imply they cannot change.

## Community Reports

Reports capture observations at a time, not guarantees or replacements for published information.

| Shared check | What to collect |
| --- | --- |
| Status | Open, closed or unsure; service checked |
| Context | When checked; at the place or contacted it |
| Practical checks, where relevant | Card payments, power, queue or waiting-time range |

Choose questions by place type, not just category:

| Place type | Checks |
| --- | --- |
| Restaurants, cafes | Kitchen serving, walk-ins accepted, queue or wait |
| Takeaways, fast food | Orders accepted, collection or drive-through operating, wait |
| Bars, pubs | Serving, entry available, queue |
| Shops | Trading, tills operating, collection counter operating |
| Fuel stations | Pumps operating, availability by fuel grade, air pump working, shop open |
| Workshops, fitment centres | Service operating, walk-ins accepted, queue or wait |
| Car washes | Operating, queue or wait |
| Vehicle testing centres | Tests running, walk-ins accepted, queue |
| Government, municipal offices | Service counter operating, systems working, queue, still accepting people |
| Post offices | Counter operating, parcel collection or posting available, systems working, queue |
| Libraries | Open, entry available, computers or printing available |
| Cinemas, theatres | Screenings or performances running, entry or ticket counter operating |
| Museums, attractions | Open, entry available, exhibits or activities accessible |
| Golf, sports facilities | Course or facility accessible, activity running, walk-ins accepted |
| MTB trails, parks, reserves | Entrance open, route open or closed, observed obstruction or closure notice |
| Climbing centres, gyms, pools | Facility operating, activity available, entry or session capacity |

Types without a question set above use the shared checks until we define relevant questions. Do not borrow questions that do not fit.

## Display And Flow Rules

- Research answers: "This service is scheduled to run."
- Reports answer: "Someone checked it 12 minutes ago."
- Keep expectations from hours separate from community observations; show the source and time.
- Ask one question per step. Let people skip what they did not check.
- Do not ask users to certify trail safety, repair quality, legal compliance or whether another report is true. Collect observations, not guarantees.
- Agree the taxonomy and questions before changing the database or survey. No data has been added under this plan.
