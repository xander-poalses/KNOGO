KNOGO STYLE TILE
Typography and colour

TYPOGRAPHY

Typeface: DM Sans
Fallback: system-ui, sans-serif

Use the same typeface throughout. The voice is direct and legible.

Weights
- Regular 400: body copy, detail and helper text
- Medium 500: short supporting copy and navigation
- SemiBold 600: metadata, labels and secondary actions
- Bold 700: use only where extra emphasis is needed
- ExtraBold 800: page titles, section titles, buttons and status labels

Type scale
- Display / page title: 36-44 px, ExtraBold, line-height 1.2-1.3
- Section title: 24-32 px, ExtraBold, line-height 1.2-1.3
- Card title: 17-28 px, ExtraBold, line-height 1.2
- Body: 14-15 px, Regular, line-height 1.4-1.5
- Meta and labels: 10-13 px, SemiBold or ExtraBold

Use uppercase for short status labels and eyebrows only. Keep body copy in sentence case.

USE

Keep green for primary actions and confirmed/open states. Use gold for attention and coral or red for warnings, errors and closed states. Pair text with background colours that keep the hierarchy clear.
