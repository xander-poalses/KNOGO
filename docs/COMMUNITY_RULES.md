# KNOGO Community Rules

- Report only information you believe is current.
- Say whether you checked in person, by phone or online.
- Add the source page for online reports.
- Do not submit repeated, false or abusive reports.
- Reports expire as live evidence after four hours. Their records remain stored.
- KNOGO keeps opposing usable reports visible as a disagreement.
- A community closure indication needs three accounts aged at least 24 hours,
  two check methods, reports spanning 15 minutes and low-risk assessments.
  It is labelled "Likely closed", not a verified closure.
- Gemini checks reporting patterns, not whether a report is true. Missing or
  inconclusive reviews cannot establish a community closure.
- KNOGO may limit reporting to prevent abuse. Venue disputes do not delete
  community reports.

These rules describe the trust implementation. The hosted database was not
running that migration at the 9 October audit. Do not present them as deployed
until deployment is approved and checked.
